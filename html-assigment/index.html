About Me

My name is Mia McGill, I am a 17, soon to be 18, on december 12, th year old collage student. I was born in Belfast, Ireland and think that ireland is one of the most beautiful places on earth. I love all kinds of media, from reading fantasy books to reading and writing poetry and watching all kinds of tv shows and movies and the one i probubly do the most, listening to music. I just love how you can get so immersed in all the diffrent kinds of media. One of my favorite quotes from one of my poems called "I write" is,
